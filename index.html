Bubble Blast – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Bubble Blast ("the game") is made by VConekt LLC ("we"). This policy explains what data the game collects and how it is used.

Playing as a guest

You can play every level without an account. Guest progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

If you create an account

  • Email address: to sign in, send verification and password-reset codes.
  • Username and avatar: shown on the leaderboard.
  • Level results (best score and stars per level): to save your progress and rank you on the leaderboard.

This data is stored securely with our backend provider Supabase and is encrypted in transit. We do not sell or share your account data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob (banners on menus and occasional full-screen ads between levels, never during play). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address) and app diagnostics, and shares them with Google. In the EEA/UK you are asked for consent first and can change it any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Remove Ads

A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off all ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all its data at any time. See Delete your account or email khakan@vconekt.com.
